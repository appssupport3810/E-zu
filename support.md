# E-zu Support

E-zu is a travel guide and trip planner for Japan: 47 prefectures, local food, seasonal events, day-by-day trips, trip posters and printable itineraries.

## Contact
Email: **apps.support3810@gmail.com**

We read every message. Tell us about a bug, a wrong place or photo, or an idea for the app. If something isn't working, please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need an account?**
No. There is no sign-in. Your trips and saved places stay on your iPhone.

**Will my trips sync to my other devices?**
Not yet. Trips are stored only on the iPhone where you made them.

**How do I see the introduction again?**
My Trip → About & privacy → How E-zu works.

**How do I show an address to a taxi driver?**
Open a place in your trip and tap "Show in Japanese". The card shows the name and address in large Japanese text.

**Why do I see different booking sites from my friend?**
E-zu shows the booking sites most people from your region use, based on your iPhone's region setting. You can change it under About → Booking sites.

**Are event dates correct?**
Festival and event dates change every year. Always check the official website before you go.

**A photo shows the wrong place.**
Please email us the place name and we'll fix it.
